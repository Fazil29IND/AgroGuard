Rice (Paddy) Pyricularia oryzae (Fungus)

Rice Blast

Top Predicted Conditions
Key Symptoms
  • Spindle-shaped foliar lesions with grayish centers and brown borders.
  • Rapid expansion causing leaf scorch and reduced photosynthesis.
Chemical Treatment
Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 1.0 g/L or Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 1.5 mL/L immediately upon symptom appearance.
Organic & Biological Control
Foliar spray of Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 10 g/L or Panchagavya 3% (30 mL/L).
Prevention & Crop Care
Split nitrogen fertilizer into 3 applications; seed treatment with Tricyclazole @ 2 g/kg; avoid prolonged moisture stress.
